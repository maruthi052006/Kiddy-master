About Us

MGR Purushothaman Matriculation School

MGR Purushothaman Matriculation School is a reputed institution located in B.Mutlur, Chidambaram, Cuddalore District, Tamil Nadu. We are committed to providing quality education that blends academic excellence with strong moral values. Our dedicated faculty, child-friendly environment, and comprehensive curriculum ensure that every child grows into a confident and capable individual.
