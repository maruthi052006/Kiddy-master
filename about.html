About Us

MGR Purushothaman Matriculation School

MGR Purushothaman Matriculation School is a well-established institution located in B.Mutlur, Chidambaram, Cuddalore District, Tamil Nadu. Founded with a vision to provide quality education to children from KG to Class V, our school has been a trusted name in the community for years. We believe every child is unique and deserves the best start in life. Our dedicated and experienced faculty, safe and nurturing environment, and strong value-based curriculum make us the preferred choice for parents in and around Chidambaram.
